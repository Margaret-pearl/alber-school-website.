Join ALBER SCHOOL and become part of our learning community.

Admission Process

1. Submit Application

Complete the admission application form and submit the required documents.

2. Assessment

The learner may be invited for a simple assessment where applicable.

3. Confirmation

Successful applicants receive an admission letter and joining instructions.

4. Welcome

Complete registration and begin your learning journey at ALBER SCHOOL.

Admission Requirements

  • ✔ Copy of Birth Certificate
  • ✔ Passport-size Photograph
  • ✔ Previous School Report (if applicable)
  • ✔ Parent/Guardian Identification
  • ✔ Completed Admission Form
